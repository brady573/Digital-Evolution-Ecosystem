# Pixel Phenotype Prototype — Evidence Package (Phase 2)

Prototype baseline: `m4b-phenotype-prototype-v1`. All numerical constants are tuning inputs, not accepted design values.
Family order, monochrome, inspection tier unless noted. `E`/`P`/`I` = ecosystem/population/inspection LOD.

Visual companion: `viewer.html` in this directory (self-contained, offline) renders every matrix, strip, pair, and dense scene on canvas, including tier toggles for scenes.

## 1. Six-family silhouette matrix (low / medium / high expression)

Expression driver per family (other traits at the family center, lineage-anchored so the family holds):
blob/speed, segmented/speed, radial/sensing, plated/metabolism, branching/sensing, paddled/speed.
Low ends are basin-limited: each family's identity needs its driver above a floor (e.g. radial needs sensing reach, plated needs metabolic intensity) — pushing below genuinely leaves the basin. The generator asserts the family holds and fails loudly otherwise.
