    What the science words mean
    Ways of life
    Technical: realized effective niches. How many distinct resource-use strategies are actually being used.
    Two stable lifestyles
    Technical: persistent dual-niche partitioning. A- and B-adapted groups eat different foods, live in matching zones, and persist through time.
    Family-line diversity
    Technical: effective lineages. How evenly the living population is spread across evolutionary family lines.
    Trait variety
    Technical: trait diversity. Variation across the six inherited traits.
    Nutrient tendency
    Technical: diet bias. An inherited tendency to get more energy from Nutrient A or Nutrient B.
    Home-zone preference
    Technical: habitat preference. An inherited tendency to search toward one nutrient zone.
    Research & technical notes
    The main screen uses everyday language. Exports keep the technical names used by the model. These sources inform experimental design, ecological niches, resource competition, diversity, and interpretation; they do not validate the prototype’s exact equations.
    • Ofria & Wilke (2004) — Avida · controlled digital-evolution experiments.
    • Dolson & Ofria (2021) · niches, competition, spatial ecology, and eco-evolutionary digital systems.
    • Cooper & Ofria (2002) · stable coexistence under competition for limiting resources.
    • Travisano et al. (1995) · replicated populations, adaptation, chance, and history.
    • Yedid, Ofria & Lenski (2009) · disturbance and recovery in digital communities.
    • Stearns (1989) · life-history tradeoffs.
    • L’Ecuyer et al. (2002) · independent RNG streams and synchronized comparisons.
    • Chao, Chiu & Jost (2014) · effective-number diversity measures.

    Clades form from mutation branches that become established and persist. Founder ancestry remains recorded underneath them; clades are a human-scale history layer, not a claim that each is a biological species.
